13Exercise 4 of 7 · Adaptation
Big idea 3 · sea level rise on the coast

Coastal impacts and adaptation strategies

Adaptation means adjusting ecological, social or economic systems in response to actual or expected climate change and its effects.

Impacts

  1. Some low-lying islands disappear.
  2. Coastal land floods and goes under water.
  3. More erosion and loss of coastal habitat.
  4. Salt water seeps into rivers and groundwater.
