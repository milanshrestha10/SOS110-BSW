2.4Prompt · 1 of 2
Assignment prompt · 1 of 2

One mind map, three sections

Objective: collaboratively analyze the ecosystem services of Grand Canyon National Park (GCNP) and evaluate how human-driven changes affect its long-term sustainability.

Time30–45 minutes TeamUp to 5 (your sign-up group) ToolsZoom Whiteboard, InsightMaker, Canva, Miro, Google Slides or similar
Task 1

Native biodiversity list

Identify the biological "engine" of the park. Use only official sources: nps.govusgs.gov

  • Plants: 3+ representative plant communities (e.g., Ponderosa pine, desert scrub)
  • Animals: 3+ native animal species (e.g., California condor, Kaibab squirrel)
Task 2

Ecosystem services mapping

A grid or web: one specific GCNP example per category, with an arrow to a sustainability outcome.

ServiceGCNP exampleOutcome / feedback ProvisioningYour unique example→Sustainability outcome RegulatingYour unique example→Sustainability outcome SupportingYour unique example→Sustainability outcome CulturalYour unique example→Sustainability outcome

Format: Provisioning: Freshwater springs → supports desert megafauna survival. Past examples (springs and megafauna, soil stabilization and river siltation) don't count: find new ones.

Task 3

Sustainability impact analysis

One team paragraph, 4–6 sentences, written on the map:

  • The challenge: one major threat (drought, invasive species, climate disruption)
  • The link: the Task 2 service it harms most
  • The human factor: how people drive it (dam management, over-tourism)
  • The solution: one viable strategy to reduce the impact
Walk through the three sections left to right; they become three labeled areas on the team's mind map. Task 1: only nps.gov or usgs.gov count as sources. Task 2 is the heart of it: four service types, one Grand Canyon example each, and an arrow to a concrete outcome. The two past examples are shown so teams know not to reuse them. Task 3 ties it together: the threat must name one service from their own Task 2. Suggest teams split up Tasks 1 and 2 for the first 15 minutes, then write Task 3 together.
