Top predators10
Energy units are teaching numbers, not to scale.
2nd law of thermodynamicsEvery time energy changes form or moves, some turns into heat that cannot do work again. So no step is 100% efficient.
Rule of thumbOnly about 10% of the energy at one level reaches the next. Plants are the base of every food chain.
1,000units reach you if you eat the grain
100units reach you if the grain is fed to cattle and you eat the beef
What it means for the Green RevolutionFeeding more people, and more meat eaters, takes a lot more plant growth. Farms pushed yields up with new seeds, water, fertilizer and fuel.
