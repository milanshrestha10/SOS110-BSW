Magnifying glass

Tap for more information

Use it on any text item, row or photo spot that has a longer explanation. The panel opens over a dimmed page and closes with the red X, a tap outside, or Esc.

Data

Facts and indicators

Use it for numbers, rates and indicators. Show up to three boxes, and label made-up numbers as teaching numbers.

Example fact
~350
Sunny days a year in Arizona.
Example indicator

Break-even time.
Caution

Document

Write and submit your answer

The document icon opens the answer panel. Inside, the Submit button carries the same icon and turns green with Deck.sent(button, "Submitted") once saved.
