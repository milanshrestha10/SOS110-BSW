2.4Prompt · 1 of 2
Assignment prompt · 1 of 2

One mind map, three sections

Objective: collaboratively analyze the ecosystem services of Grand Canyon National Park (GCNP) and evaluate how human-driven changes affect its long-term sustainability.

Time30–45 minutes TeamUp to 5, your sign-up group ToolsZoom Whiteboard, InsightMaker, Canva, Miro, Google Slides SourcesNPS.gov and USGS.gov
Task 1

Ecosystem services mapping

A grid or table: one specific, new GCNP example per category, linked to a sustainability outcome.

ServiceGCNP exampleOutcome / feedback ProvisioningYour example→Outcome RegulatingYour example→Outcome SupportingYour example→Outcome CulturalYour example→Outcome

Past examples (springs and megafauna, soil stabilization and river siltation) don't count: find new ones.

Task 2

Diagram

Turn the Task 1 links into a diagram of how the park's ecosystem services work together.

  • Build from Task 1: every service and link from your grid appears here
  • Show the park at work: connect services to GCNP's native plants and animals (e.g., Ponderosa pine, desert scrub, California condor, Kaibab squirrel)
  • Show outcomes both ways: mark positive (+) and negative (−) sustainability outcomes
Task 3

Sustainability impact analysis

One team paragraph, 4–6 sentences, written on the map:

  • The challenge: one major threat (drought, invasive species, climate disruption)
  • The link: the Task 1 service it harms most
  • The human factor: how people drive it (dam management, over-tourism)
  • The solution: at least one strategy to reduce the impact
