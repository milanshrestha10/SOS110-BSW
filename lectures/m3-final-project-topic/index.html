M3Sample topic
Sample topic

The Impact of Urban Green Spaces on Community Well-Being

In this project, I will explore how urban green spaces, such as parks and community gardens, contribute to the overall well-being of city residents (Jennings et al 2024). I plan to investigate the psychological and physical health benefits associated with access to these green areas, including reduced stress levels, increased physical activity, and improved social cohesion among community members (Lampert et al 2021; Lin et al 2025). By analyzing peer-reviewed studies and case examples from cities that have successfully integrated green spaces into their urban planning, I aim to demonstrate the importance of prioritizing green infrastructure in sustainable city development. This topic not only highlights the environmental aspect of sustainability but also emphasizes its social implications, making it a compelling area of research.
